Dynamic Pricing Agent

An agent that watches competitor prices — and other signals — and adjusts a product's price automatically. It won't let a price cut push the profit margin below 20%. If a proposed change would cross that line, the agent pauses and asks a person to approve or reject it before anything goes live.
